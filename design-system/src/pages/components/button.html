Components

Button

Compact by design — the default is 32px tall, not 40px. A group screen carries a lot of small actions and full-size buttons would crowd every row. One filled primary per screen; everything else is quieter.

Variants

default
The one thing to do on this screen. Never two on the same view.
secondary
A real alternative alongside the primary — "Save draft" next to "Save".
outline
Standalone actions in a header or toolbar, and dropdown triggers.
ghost
Navigation-ish actions inside a section heading: "See all", "Settle up".
destructive
A 10% tint with destructive text — never a solid red fill.
link
Inline in prose, where a button-shaped thing would break the sentence.

Icon-only buttons always carry an aria-label. The xs and sm sizes also step the radius down to --radius-md so a short button doesn't turn into a lozenge.

With icons

Icons are 16px lucide strokes (12px at xs, 14px at sm), marked aria-hidden, and inherit colour. A leading icon names the action; a trailing icon names the destination.

States

A submitting button keeps its label and adds a spinner. It never collapses to a bare spinner — people lose track of what they pressed.

Rules

Do

Wrap a Link with asChild when the action navigates. It should be a real anchor: middle-click, long-press, and "open in new tab" all have to work.

Don't

Put a second filled primary on the screen, or use destructive for anything reversible. Archiving a group is outline; deleting it is destructive.
