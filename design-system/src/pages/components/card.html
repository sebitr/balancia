Components

Card

A ringed surface for one coherent block of information. Padding comes from a single --card-spacing variable, so size="sm" tightens header, content, and footer together rather than each being overridden by hand.

Rules

Do

Give a card one job. If it needs two headings inside it, it is two cards — or it is a list.

Don't

Wrap a plain list of expenses in a card. Use divide-y rounded-lg border: the ring plus the row borders is one hairline too many.
