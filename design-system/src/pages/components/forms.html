Components

Forms

Every control is 32px tall and shares one focus and one invalid treatment. Labels sit above, descriptions under the label, errors under the control — and errors are announced, not just coloured.

Field

Entered in the expense's own currency; conversion happens on save.

Validation

Enter an amount greater than zero.
Shares total €120.00 but the expense is €128.40 — €8.40 is unassigned.

Errors say what is wrong and give the number needed to fix it. "Invalid amount" is not an error message. FieldError carries role="alert", so it reaches a screen reader the moment it appears.

States

Inputs are text-base below the md breakpoint and text-sm above it. That is deliberate: iOS Safari zooms the viewport on focus for anything under 16px.

Rules

Do

Set inputmode="decimal" on amounts, and keep the currency next to the field it applies to.

Don't

Use placeholder text as the label. It disappears exactly when someone is filling the field in.
