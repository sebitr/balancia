Brand

Splitting a bill is division. A dot for what was spent, a rule to divide it, a pan below that catches everyone's share. Three parts, one width, nothing decorative. Ships as src/components/brand/wordmark.tsx; the rule and pan take currentColor, the dot is always --primary.

Clear space on every side is 1×, the diameter of the dot. Below 20px the pan and the rule merge into one shape — use the mono mark at those sizes and let the dot carry the colour.

The mark takes two colours and no more: currentColor for the rule and pan, --primary for the dot. No gradients, no shadows, no outlines, no third colour.
