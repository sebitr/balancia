Foundations

Motion

Motion here confirms that something was pressed or that a surface arrived. It never decorates and it never delays. If an animation would make a person wait to see a balance, it is wrong.

Durations

150ms
The default. Colour, border, and shadow transitions on every interactive element.
instant
The 1px press translate — it must feel mechanical, not animated.
200ms
Dialog, sheet, and popover enter/exit (fade + small scale or slide).
2s
Skeleton pulse, looping. Slow enough to read as "loading", not as a flicker.

Press

Buttons translate down 1px while active. Menu triggers (aria-haspopup) are excluded — the surface they open is the feedback, and double motion reads as a glitch.

Focus

One focus treatment everywhere: the border becomes --ring and a 3px ring at 50% opacity sits outside it. Tab into these.

Loading

Skeletons mirror the shape of what is arriving — a title line, a metadata line, a row. A generic grey block tells nobody anything.

Reduced motion

prefers-reduced-motion: reduce collapses every duration to 0.01ms and stops looping animations after one iteration. This is a blanket rule, not a per-component opt-in — new components inherit it for free.

Do

Animate colour, opacity, and transform. They stay off the layout path and stay at 60fps on a mid-range phone.

Don't

Animate height, width, or position of anything containing an amount. A number that slides while you read it is worse than no animation.
