Spacing, radius & elevation

One radius root drives the whole scale, and elevation is deliberately almost absent — surfaces separate with a hairline, not a shadow. Shadows are reserved for things that genuinely float above the page.

Radius

--radius: 0.75rem. Every other step is a multiplier, so re-rounding the whole product is a one-line change.

×0.6 — checkbox, inline code

×0.8 — small buttons, skeletons

×1 — buttons, inputs, lists
xl · 1.05rem
×1.4 — cards, dialogs, empty states

Spacing

0.75rem
List row padding, gap between sibling controls, card gap at size="sm".
1rem
Card padding (--card-spacing), page gutter on mobile.
1.25rem
Gap between fields in a form group.
1.5rem
Gap between page sections — the largest vertical rhythm on a screen.
max-w-3xl
Content column cap in the app shell. Everything centres inside it.
pb-28
Main padding when a bottom nav is present, so the last row clears the bar.

The bottom nav also carries pb-[env(safe-area-inset-bottom)]. Anything fixed to the bottom edge does, or it sits under a phone's home indicator.

Cards separate with a 1px ring at 10% foreground. In dark mode a shadow would be invisible anyway; a ring works in both themes.

Raised — floats only
Settle up

shadow-raised

--shadow-raised is for dialogs, popovers, dropdowns, and toasts — surfaces that are genuinely above the page. --shadow-card is lighter still and is used only on the switch thumb.

Rules

Do

Separate stacked content with a divider inside a bordered container (divide-y rounded-lg border). It is quieter than a stack of cards and scans faster.

Don't

Nest a card inside a card, or give a card a shadow to imply importance. Importance comes from position on the page — your own balance goes first.
