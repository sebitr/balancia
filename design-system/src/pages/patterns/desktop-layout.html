Patterns

Desktop layout

The app is drawn for a phone, and a desktop window gets the same screens with the room used modestly: a sidebar in place of the header and the bottom bar, a group's places as tabs under its name, two columns on the one screen that has two kinds of thing to say, and every other screen left at the one readable column it has always had. Entering a month of receipts is a desk task; nothing else here is a reason to redesign a screen.

#438 first used the width with a 15rem rail inside a group — the switcher, Add, the four places and the account. The sidebar replaced it: the places moved into the group's header, and everything else went onto every signed-in screen, Home included.

Three widths

below md
The phone. The bottom bar, the header along the top, one column, and every type step a point larger. Nothing on this page applies.
md to lg
768–1023px, a tablet held upright. The desk type scale, still the bar and the top header — it is held like a phone. Bottom sheets stop at a phone's width from here.
lg up
1024px and wider. The sidebar on every signed-in screen — Home and Notifications too — in place of the header and the bar; a group's header and tabs above its four places; on the overview, two columns. Settings stays a surface of its own with its ✕, and no sidebar — its hub open beside the screen it led to.
xl up
1280px and wider. Everything as drawn: 40px gutters, the group's tile 48px and its name a step larger. From 1536px the wide column stops at 75rem and centres.

The overview, from lg up

The money on the left — where you stand, the balances, the repayments that square the group. The context on the right — what changed since your last visit, what the group has spent, the way into Activity. The left column comes first in the document, so a screen reader, a keyboard and a phone all read it in the order the single column always had.

width
The screen widens to --app-content-max (75rem, gutters included) for a layout marked data-layout="wide" — the overview's two columns, the transactions table and Home's two columns from xl — and only for one, which is exactly what the expanded sidebar leaves at 1440px. 24px between the overview's columns; the blocks inside each keep the overview's own gap. Home's columns are drawn on its own page.
columns
Halves at lg: at 1024 the sidebar leaves about 740px, and 360px each is what the balance rows' three columns and the spending card's category rows need. Seven to five from xl (minmax(0, 7fr) minmax(0, 5fr)): at 1440 the money gets about 640px and the context about 460px. The left column is the money at every width — first in the document, so the phone's reading order survives — and is never the narrower one.
group header
Above the screen, outside the part that moves, at the screen's own width — the readable column, or the wide one under the overview. 28px above it, 20px to the tabs, 24px from the tabs to the screen.
empty group
One column at every width. "Start here" is two steps, not a dashboard.
position strip
Hangs from the top of the window beside the sidebar, at whichever of its widths, since there is no header above it; it fades in once the hero has scrolled past the group header and out of sight.

Every other screen

Notifications, People, Settings, an entry, a person: one column, max-w-3xl, centred in the space beside the sidebar, never stretched across it. Transactions is a table at this width instead, with its filters on one row — see Expense timeline. Home keeps that column up to xl and then sets "Recent in your groups" beside the groups — see Your groups. Gutters open to 24px at lg and 40px at xl. An entry's Edit and Remove, docked above the bar on a phone, sit in the screen's header at this width (board 08).

Settings, from lg up

Still a surface — no rail, no wordmark, no bell — with the room used for one thing: the hub stays open beside the screen it led to. Its rows down the left, each with its value as a phone shows it; the chosen screen on the right, exactly as it is on a phone; the ✕ once, along the top of both, closing back to wherever settings was opened from.

Appearance & language

The screen as it is on a phone: its cards, its controls, its silence when a control flicks back.

widths
The surface stops at max-w-280 (70rem) and centres, with 24px gutters at lg and 40px from xl. The pane is 20rem: it holds the phone's own rows, tile and chevron included, which were drawn for a phone's column. 32px between the panes; the screen takes the rest.
the pane
The hub itself, read once and drawn twice — the phone's page below lg, this pane above, each display: none at the other's widths. One navigation landmark named "Settings". The row whose screen is on the right carries aria-current="page" and the wash a step past the hover's; rows swap the screen without the push a phone's rows make, as a tab does.
the screen
Unchanged, minus what the surface took over: no arrow back to the hub beside it, so its title leads at text-2xl; no ✕ of its own; no gutter; no sticky header. Nothing sticks at this width — it is a page that scrolls.
/settings
Shows the first screen, the account, beside the pane rather than an empty half, and lights the account at the top of the pane.
keyboard
A skip link past the ✕ and the pane to the screen, parked above the top edge as the app shell's is.

What gives way first at 1024px

1
Second columns wrap under the first. The overview's two stay side by side, as halves of about 360px each rather than seven to five.
2
Gutters close to 24px, the group's tile to 36px and its name to text-xl.
3
The sidebar is last. It stays expanded at 1024 — it is the switcher, and the only place every balance shows at once — unless the reader folds it, which they can at any width.

Sheets

From md up a bottom sheet is max-w-md (28rem) wide and centred under the window, the way a dialog is, instead of a strip across all of it. It still rises from the bottom edge, still pins its actions under a scrolling body, and still dismisses the same way. A sheet that wants another width says md:max-w-*. The entry drawer's loading skeleton is held to the same width, so the drawer does not change shape when the form arrives.

From lg up the entry drawer and the group chooser are not sheets at all but dialogs — see The entry dialog. They are the same element at both widths, asked for with desk on SheetContent, so a window resized across 1024px keeps what was typed. A sheet raised from inside the dialog — who pays whom, every payment method, the schedule — is a 30rem dialog over it.

The entry dialog

Adding or changing an entry, on a desk. A dialog over the screen it was started from, which stays where it was — the same intercepted route as the phone's drawer, so Back closes it, and so do Esc, the scrim, the ✕ and Cancel; what was typed is the draft whichever way it closed. 720px wide at lg and 880px from xl (dialog-size.ts), anchored by its top so a form that grows pushes only its foot down, and capped at the window less 5rem, past which the body scrolls between a fixed title row and a fixed foot.

title row
The title, the three kinds of entry as tabs each as wide as its word, and the ✕. Below lg the tabs are their own full-width row, as ever.
top
The amount (18.75rem) beside the description, its category and the date from xl; at lg they stack, the amount first. The same cards as the phone's.
split
The split sheet's own contents, in place, with no row to open and no Done: who paid, who is in — the reader "You" and first — the four methods in full beside their hint, and the shares in two columns.
pickers
Category and currency open as popovers on the row and the chip that open them; the currency list searches as it opens. Who pays whom, every payment method and the schedule stay sheets, drawn as 30rem dialogs.
⌘↵
Ctrl ↵ off a Mac. The form's own key, not the window's: it presses the button, from any field, and does nothing the button would not. The button says so with aria-keyshortcuts; the hint beside it is hidden from a screen reader.
repayment
The outstanding repayments one line each — the mark, the face, the sentence, the figure — then the amount with Full and Part of it under it, beside the description, the date and how it was paid as chips. The outcome sits in a box with the currency the two end up square in, and a line on Balancia only recording it. Settle up's Record a repayment opens this tab with nobody picked.
which group
Add expense outside a group asks first, in a 30rem dialog: each row a tile, the name, its people and last activity, and a number key. 1 to 9 pick, ↑ ↓ walk the rows, ↵ takes one; a digit typed into the search stays a digit.

Keys

From lg up the shell answers a handful of keys, from one listener mounted once beside the screen (src/components/layout/use-shortcuts.ts). Every key presses a control that is already on the screen — the sidebar's Search, its Add expense and its fold toggle carry a data-shortcut for it — and none is the only way to anything. Below lg there is no sidebar and no key does anything.

⌘ K
Search or jump to: the command palette, from any screen and from inside a field. Ctrl K off a Mac — and only there, because Ctrl K in a Mac's text field deletes to the end of the line. Pressed again, it closes the palette. Under another dialog it does nothing.
N
Add expense: to this group inside one, through "Add to which group?" outside. It presses the sidebar's own Add, so the offline form and the chooser come with it.
S
Settle up in this group. Nothing outside a group.
⌘ \
Folds the sidebar to its icons, or opens it again, remembered for the device as a click on the toggle is.
Esc
Closes the topmost thing — a menu, then the palette, then the dialog under it. It is each layer's own, never the shell's: a second listener would close the palette under an open menu.
↑ ↓ ↵
Move and follow inside the palette; ⌘ ↵ opens a row in a new tab. See Navigation › Command palette.
Single-key shortcuts — Settings › Appearance & language, from lg up
Single-key shortcuts
N adds an expense and S settles up when no field has the cursor. Stays on this device.
letters
N and S are single characters, so they fire only when nothing is being typed — no field, select, editable region or list with its own typeahead has the focus — and nothing is open over the screen. A switch turns them off (WCAG 2.1.4): a reader who drives the page by voice can say a letter without meaning a command. Keys with a modifier are not covered and stay on.
device
The switch is kept per device in a cookie, like the dark surface and the folded sidebar, so it is drawn the way it stands. On is the absence of a choice. It saves in silence, as every switch in settings does.

Rules

Do

Use the width where a screen has two kinds of thing to say, and leave a list a list. A balance row 900px wide puts the figure a hand's width from the name it belongs to.

Don't

Change anything below lg to make room for the desktop. The phone is where the app is used; every class this layout adds is lg:, or md: for a sheet. And never let the accent paint a balance in the sidebar: a position is words in the tone's ink.
