Patterns

Desktop layout

The app is drawn for a phone, and a desktop window gets the same screens with the room used modestly: a rail in place of the bottom bar, two columns on the one screen that has two kinds of thing to say, and every other screen left at the one readable column it has always had. Entering a month of receipts is a desk task; nothing else here is a reason to redesign a screen.

Three widths

below md
The phone. The bottom bar, the header along the top, one column, and every type step a point larger. Nothing on this page applies.
md to lg
768–1023px, a tablet held upright. The desk type scale, still the bar and the top header — it is held like a phone. Bottom sheets stop at a phone's width from here.
lg up
1024px and wider. Inside a group, the rail; on the overview, two columns. The dashboard and the other screens with nothing to navigate between keep the header along the top. Settings keeps its hub open beside the screen it led to.

The overview, from lg up

The money on the left — where you stand, the balances, the repayments that square the group. The context on the right — what changed since your last visit, what the group has spent, the way into Activity. The left column comes first in the document, so a screen reader, a keyboard and a phone all read it in the order the single column always had.

width
The screen widens to max-w-5xl for a layout marked data-layout="wide", and only for one. 24px between the columns; the blocks inside each keep the overview's own gap.
empty group
One column at every width. "Start here" is two steps, not a dashboard.
position strip
Hangs from the top of the window beside the rail, across the wide column, since there is no header above it.

Every other screen

Transactions, People, Settings, an entry, a person: one column, max-w-3xl, centred in the space beside the rail, never stretched across it. Gutters open to 24px at lg and 32px at xl. Anything that was docked above the bar — an entry's Edit and Remove — docks at the foot of the window instead, starting where the rail stops.

Settings, from lg up

Still a surface — no rail, no wordmark, no bell — with the room used for one thing: the hub stays open beside the screen it led to. Its rows down the left, each with its value as a phone shows it; the chosen screen on the right, exactly as it is on a phone; the ✕ once, along the top of both, closing back to wherever settings was opened from.

Appearance & language

The screen as it is on a phone: its cards, its controls, its silence when a control flicks back.

widths
The surface stops at max-w-280 (70rem) and centres, with 24px gutters at lg and 40px from xl. The pane is 20rem: it holds the phone's own rows, tile and chevron included, which were drawn for a phone's column. 32px between the panes; the screen takes the rest.
the pane
The hub itself, read once and drawn twice — the phone's page below lg, this pane above, each display: none at the other's widths. One navigation landmark named "Settings". The row whose screen is on the right carries aria-current="page" and the wash a step past the hover's; rows swap the screen without the push a phone's rows make, as a tab does.
the screen
Unchanged, minus what the surface took over: no arrow back to the hub beside it, so its title leads at text-2xl; no ✕ of its own; no gutter; no sticky header. Nothing sticks at this width — it is a page that scrolls.
/settings
Shows the first screen, the account, beside the pane rather than an empty half, and lights the account at the top of the pane.
keyboard
A skip link past the ✕ and the pane to the screen, parked above the top edge as the group rail's is.

Sheets

From md up a bottom sheet is max-w-md (28rem) wide and centred under the window, the way a dialog is, instead of a strip across all of it. It still rises from the bottom edge, still pins its actions under a scrolling body, and still dismisses the same way. A sheet that wants another width says md:max-w-*. The entry drawer's loading skeleton is held to the same width, so the drawer does not change shape when the form arrives.

Rules

Do

Use the width where a screen has two kinds of thing to say, and leave a list a list. A balance row 900px wide puts the figure a hand's width from the name it belongs to.

Don't

Change anything below lg to make room for the desktop. The phone is where the app is used; every class this layout adds is lg:, or md: for a sheet.
