Order, and why

1 — Your position
One card per currency, size="large". The question people opened the app to answer.
2 — Total spending
Chips, one per currency. Context for the numbers below, never a single summed figure across currencies.
3 — Balances
One row per person, most owed first. The verb sits under the name in the muted label style — "owes", "gets back" — and the reader's own row says "You", never their own name cut short. The figure is unsigned, in the tone's ink, because the word has already said which way it goes; the centred bar shows the group's shape. A settled person reads "Settled up", not a zero. Name and verb are one message with two tags, so French says "Tu / récupères" rather than "Toi / récupères".
3b — Suggested repayments
The transfers that clear the group, in the settle-up screen's sentences from the reader's side: "You pay Marta back", "Sam pays you back", "Yusuf pays Marta back". The figure takes the settle-up screen's colours — out, in, or neutral between two others. Every row opens something, so every row has a chevron: the reader's own debt goes straight to the repayment form, anybody else's to a sheet.
4 — Recent expenses
Five rows and a "See all". Enough to recognise the group, not enough to scroll.
5 — Activity
What changed, for people returning after a few days, under "Since your last visit" with a "View all" beside the heading. That block renders only when something is new, so the screen also ends on an Activity row — icon, title, caption, chevron, drawn like the Statistics row — that is there regardless. It is the way to the screen every delete dialog promises a deletion can be restored from, and it is shown to everyone who can open the overview, guests included. On an empty group it appears only once something has been deleted there: a brand-new group keeps its two first steps, and a group emptied by deleting its only expense still has the way back. Settings lists the same screen under Shortcuts.

When the group has no expenses the whole overview collapses to this. Showing empty balance cards with "settled up" would be technically true and completely useless.

The other two routes in belong here, not only in settings. Someone arriving from Splitwise has a file in hand and no reason to guess that importing is possible; someone who created the group alone needs people before a split means anything. Each is shown only when the viewer may actually do it — a guest sees the first button by itself.
