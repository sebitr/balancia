A person's page

Tap a name in the overview's balances and you land on that person. The page answers three questions in order: what is between the two of you, what you can do about it, and which entries made it — and only then the statistics. A figure that says "You owe Marta" and offers nothing to do about it sends the reader to another screen to find the same number again.

When there is nothing to do

Settled up
"You and Marta are settled up", and no buttons.
Your own page
Headed "Your balance in this group". No buttons — there is nobody to pay — and the row reads "Your entries".
No place in the group
A reader with no participant row of their own is a party to nothing: "Marta's balance in this group", and no buttons.
A guest
A full financial participant, offered exactly what the settle screen offers them.
Several currencies
One card per currency, each with its own buttons. Never a total.

People at width — from lg up

From 1024px the People card is a table over the same people, in the same order: who they are, their role, what they paid and what their share came to, where they stand, and what the reader can do about them. Not a second list — the menu at the end of a row opens the very panel the phone opens under it, and a link made there is revealed there. The name opens the person's page and the row is its link, as in the transactions table.

Group link

The group link card, exactly as on a phone and in settings.

columns
Laid out to what they hold: every column but the person's is as wide as its widest cell and never wraps, so a figure is never cut short; the person's column takes the rest and is the one that truncates. Under 48rem of table — a 1024px window beside an expanded sidebar — Share goes first; the person's page has it.
position
One line per currency the person is not square in, the figure and its word in the tone's ink and the figure unsigned — "€99.40 owes", "€248.00 you get back" on the reader's own row. "Settled up" in the neutral ink, never a zero. Each line is one message, so French can say "doit 99,40 €".
paid, share
In one currency, the one the group spends in most often (a converted group's base currency first); the others are named in the footer. Read off the balances the screen already loads. Spending only.
actions
Remind, for whoever owes the reader — the settle screen's own button, "Reminded" for a day after. Then ⋯, "Options for Tomás": the phone's open row in a popover — name and address, the personal link, Remove from group, Leave this group — or no button at all when that panel would be empty. A row whose menu is open is washed a step darker than a hovered one, never in the accent: it carries a balance.
group link
Beside the table once the screen is 64rem wide, under it until then. Placed by the grid, never moved in the document.

Rules

Do

Give every figure the width it needs. Paid, share and entries are rows, label left and figure right, so "CHF 12,345.67" fits at 360px and anything wider wraps. Labels wrap too.

Don't

Truncate an amount. "CHF 3,066.…" is not a shorter figure, it is a wrong one — the three tiles this replaced did exactly that at 375px.

Do

Say it the way a person would: "Marta's balance in this group", "Not settled with 2 people", "Marta paid 2.8 times their share in this period", "Smallest share of 3 people".

Don't

Write like an analyst: "Net across the group", "Open with 2 people", "median member", a ratio to two decimals.
