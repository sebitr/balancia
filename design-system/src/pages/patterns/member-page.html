A person's page

Tap a name in the overview's balances and you land on that person. The page answers three questions in order: what is between the two of you, what you can do about it, and which entries made it — and only then the statistics. A figure that says "You owe Marta" and offers nothing to do about it sends the reader to another screen to find the same number again.

When there is nothing to do

Settled up
"You and Marta are settled up", and no buttons.
Your own page
Headed "Your balance in this group". No buttons — there is nobody to pay — and the row reads "Your entries".
No place in the group
A reader with no participant row of their own is a party to nothing: "Marta's balance in this group", and no buttons.
A guest
A full financial participant, offered exactly what the settle screen offers them.
Several currencies
One card per currency, each with its own buttons. Never a total.

Rules

Do

Give every figure the width it needs. Paid, share and entries are rows, label left and figure right, so "CHF 12,345.67" fits at 360px and anything wider wraps. Labels wrap too.

Don't

Truncate an amount. "CHF 3,066.…" is not a shorter figure, it is a wrong one — the three tiles this replaced did exactly that at 375px.

Do

Say it the way a person would: "Marta's balance in this group", "Not settled with 2 people", "Marta paid 2.8 times their share in this period", "Smallest share of 3 people".

Don't

Write like an analyst: "Net across the group", "Open with 2 people", "median member", a ratio to two decimals.
