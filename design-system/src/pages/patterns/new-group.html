Patterns

New group

A group is a set of people sharing costs, so creating one asks who they are. This is the moment where "they don't need an account" stops being a claim on a marketing page and becomes something you watch happen: you type three names, and three people are in the group.

On a phone

Who else is in this group?
  • Amélie You
  • Jonas
  • Ravi

They do not need an account. Add them now and send whoever wants to use the app a link later — you can add or rename people at any time.

Your own name heads the list, labelled You. Without it the list reads as "the other people", and the count never matches what the group overview will show a moment later.

What the copy has to carry

  • "Who else is in this group?" A question, not a heading. It expects an answer.
  • "They do not need an account." Stated where it is true, not on a help page.
  • "…a link later" Names the next step without demanding it now.
  • "…at any time" Removes the fear of getting the list wrong.

Rules

Do

Let the field stay empty. Someone creating a group alone, or importing from Splitwise a minute later, should not have to dismiss anything.

Don't

Ask for email addresses here, or make Enter submit the form. The first turns a thirty-second task into recruitment; the second creates a group when someone meant to add a third person.
