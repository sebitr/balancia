New group

A group is a set of people sharing costs, so creating one asks who they are. It also asks the one thing that can never be changed afterwards: which currency the balance is kept in. That answer comes straight after the name, already guessed from where the phone is, and the button that fixes it says it out loud.

Your own name heads the list, labelled You. Without it the list reads as "the other people", and the count never matches what the group overview will show a moment later. The list grows downwards, under the currency, so however many people are added the currency and its button are both on screen at 375 × 812.

Where the currency comes from

Picked by hand
Final. Nothing that arrives afterwards — the device answering late at hydration included — replaces it.
Preferred currency
What the account said it prefers, in its settings.
Where the device is
The time zone the sheet already states in its footer, mapped to what is paid there: Europe/Zurich → CHF, Europe/London → GBP, Asia/Tokyo → JPY. Never the language: en-US is set on phones far from America.
EUR
Only when none of the above says anything — a device on UTC.

The same guess decides the currency of a group started without an account, from the welcome screen.

What the copy has to carry

  • "Create group in CHF" The tap that fixes the currency says what it fixes. Plain "Create group" when there is a balance per currency.
  • "Fixed once the group exists." Beside the answer it is about, not at the bottom of the sheet.
  • "No account needed." Stated where it is true, not on a help page.
  • "…add or rename people later on" Removes the fear of getting the list wrong.

Rules

Do

Ask the currency straight after the name, and guess it from where the device is. Let the people field stay empty: someone creating a group alone, or importing from Splitwise a minute later, should not have to dismiss anything.

Don't

Put the currency where a growing list can push it under the footer, or make Enter submit the form. The first creates a group in a currency nobody saw; the second creates one when someone meant to add a third person.
