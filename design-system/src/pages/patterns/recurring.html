Recurring expenses

A recurring expense is a property of an entry, not a kind of its own: it is set up in the entry form by turning Repeats on, changed there too, and listed on a screen of its own that Transactions leads to. Its schedule reads one way everywhere it is shown.

subtitle
"Added on their day, automatically. Days follow the group's time zone, Europe/Zurich." — said to a person, not a log line.
row menu
Edit, then Pause or Resume, then Delete. Edit first, because changing the rent or the split is what a rule is opened for most often. Pause and Delete raise an Undo; Edit opens a form.
add
One primary button under the list, and the empty state's action when there is no list. It opens the entry form with Repeats already on — the screen has no form of its own. From lg up it stands at the end of the title's row instead (see the table below).
back
To Transactions, the way in; to group settings when the settings shortcut was the way in.

Description · Repeats · Amount · Next · the row's menu. Decided by the table's own box, not the window: under 56rem of table — a 1024px window beside the expanded sidebar — Repeats and Next fold into the description's second line in the phone's words, "Every month on the 1st · Next: 1 Nov 2026 · 10 generated so far".
next
Headed Next, so a date stands bare in it. A paused rule says "Paused — nothing will be generated" there, and a series with no dates left "No further occurrences" — never a date that will not come.
amount
A cost on a schedule, not anybody's balance: plain ink, no sign, no tone, right-aligned and tabular.
row
Not a link: a rule has no screen of its own, it is changed in the entry form. The menu — the phone's, offering the same items — is the one control in it. Hover --wash-1; while its menu is open, --wash-2, as an open row of People — not the accent, since the row holds an amount. A paused rule is muted as well as badged.

How many are running and paused, and what an edit does to the entries already added — the same promise the edit's toast makes.
add
At the end of the title's row, and none under the table: the table can run longer than the window. The empty state keeps the readable column and its own action.

One sentence per schedule

The repeat sheet's hero and presets, the entry form's Repeat row and the list above all say a rule with one family of messages, recurring.schedule. The day of the month is an ordinal in English and plain in French; the interval is a plural; the weekday is the locale's own name for it.

Rule English French
monthly, day 1 Every month on the 1st Tous les mois, le 1er
every 3 months, day 22 Every 3 months on the 22nd Tous les 3 mois, le 22
monthly, 2nd Tuesday Every month on the second Tuesday Tous les mois, le deuxième mardi
every 2 weeks, Friday Every 2 weeks on Friday Toutes les 2 semaines, le vendredi
yearly Every year on 15 Oct Tous les ans, le 15 oct.
daily Every day Tous les jours

Changing one

the form
The entry form, titled Edit recurring expense, opened on the rule. Expense and Income only; Repeats on and fixed — a rule stays a rule, and the repeat sheet has no Don't repeat. Scan, voice and the duplicate note are for new entries and are not shown.
the button
Save for future expenses (…income). It touches the entries still to come and never the ones already added, and says so rather than "Save changes".
the date row
Shows the rule's next date. It opens on the start of the period that date falls in — its week, or its month — so an unchanged schedule keeps its date and a moved day moves within that month: never a second entry in the month already paid, never a month skipped. It cannot go earlier than the day after the last entry, nor before today.
the confirmation
"Rent updated from 1 Nov 2026. Entries already added stay as they were." A paused rule says it is still paused.

The way in from Transactions

Repeating · 2

At the end of the kind chips whenever the group has a recurring expense, paused ones included; the number counts the running ones and drops out when there are none. With no chips — a group of expenses only — it stands on its own under the title. It wraps to a line of its own rather than cut a chip short, which in French at 360px it does.

Do

Draw it as the screen's links are — link ink, a chevron, no fill — because it goes somewhere.

Don't

Make it a fourth chip. A chip filters the list in place and stays pressed; this leaves the screen.
