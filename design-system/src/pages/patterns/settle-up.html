Settle up

The end of the story. Balancia proposes the fewest repayments that clear the group, phrased as sentences a person can act on — "Ravi pays you back €99.40", "Pay Amélie back €148.60" — not as a matrix anyone has to decode. It never moves money, so every button records something the reader has already done, or asks for it.

the hero
The reader's own position in this currency — never the size of the plan, which counts payments they are neither making nor receiving. Direction three ways: the eyebrow's word, the arrow, the tone's ink.
yours
"Pay Amélie back" when the reader owes, "Ravi pays you back" when they are owed, each with its one action: I paid Amélie (and how Amélie likes to be paid, above it), or I was paid back beside Remind. The accent button is only ever on a row the reader is a party to.
not your concern
"Tomás pays Amélie", in the neutral ink. The sentence is the control: it opens the repayment form with the pair chosen ("Record for them"). Never a reminder — nobody chases a debt on someone else's behalf.

At width

From lg (1024px) each currency is a card and they stand side by side, so a debt in francs is never below the fold of a credit in euros. The first currency on the left; the rest, then the note on how a repayment works, on the right — the order a phone reads them in, where they stack and the note is not drawn. Each column is about a phone's width at 1024px, which is what its rows were drawn for.

the heading
On a phone the currency is read out, never seen — the hero's figure names it. Beside a second column it is the column's heading, drawn as a chip opposite the eyebrow.

Each column counts its own repayments. The header says only that each currency is settled on its own: the desktop board's "5 repayments clear the group" over two currencies is the cross-currency total settle-up-screen.tsx declines to state.

Opened on what is outstanding now. A partial payment is fine — the remainder stays owed.

Every button on the settle screen opens this form with the pair already chosen — "I paid Ravi", "I was paid back", "Record for them". The amount is editable. People pay round numbers and pay in parts, and a system that only accepts the exact suggested figure gets abandoned the first time somebody hands over €100 for a €99.40 debt.

Settled up

A cleared currency says "Settled up" — never "€0.00". Zero is a state, not an amount, and phrasing it removes any doubt about whether the number failed to load.

Rules

Do

Write each repayment as a sentence. "Tomás → Amélie" needs a key; "Tomás pays Amélie" does not, and "Ravi pays you back" says which of the two is the reader.

Don't

Word a button as an instruction that moves money — "Pay Ravi" — or block a payment that doesn't match the suggestion. Balancia records what people did; it does not instruct them.
