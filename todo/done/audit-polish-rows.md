# Small truths on the home screen, the transactions strip, the reminder sheet and the activity line

Merged: 2026-10-06 in #436

From the UX audit of 2026-10-05: a group with no expenses no longer reads
"Settled", the home rows say when a figure is rounded, the owing section is
called "You owe", the invite sheet stops naming its own reader, a one-category
strip goes, the reminder's activity toggle becomes a switch and its "replace
my message" question the app's own, a repayment in Activity names who paid
whom, and the guest "Start a group" screen shows its currency.
