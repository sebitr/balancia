# On a desktop, settle each currency side by side, and read an expense with its split as a table and Edit and Delete at the top

Merged: 2026-10-07 in #447

Package 6 of the desktop experience. From `lg` up: Settle up puts one currency
per column, each a card headed by its currency, with the note on what a
repayment does beside them; an expense's header carries its date, who paid and
its category, and its Edit and Delete; the split is a table of share, paid and
what the entry did to each person, with the notes and files beside it from
`xl`. A repayment's screen gets the same header. Nothing changes below `lg`.
