Package 1 of the desktop experience. From `lg` (1024px) up every signed-in
screen — Home and Notifications as well as a group — gets a sidebar in place of
#438's rail: Search (inert until the palette), Add expense, Home,
Notifications, every group with the reader's position in words and one line
per currency, and the account. It folds to a 4rem column of icons, kept per
device. A group's four places move into a header above the screen, under the
group's tile and name. Below `lg` nothing moves, except that the phone's
switcher now says each currency's direction on its own line.
