# Notifications, signing in and opening a group's link use the width of a desktop window

Branch: `feat/desktop-notifications-signin-join`

Design: https://claude.ai/artifact/Nwc4hfpwGTdcHj4e7suBHQ (boards 03 · Notifications,
19 · Sign in and 22 · Signed out on the group link)

Package 11 of the desktop experience. From `lg` (1024px) up, and nothing below
it: Notifications gets its unread line, labelled buttons, each day's rows in a
card and the reminder as a card that says who sent it and what they ask for;
the sign-in screens stand beside a plum panel with the wordmark and the
instance's name; and somebody opening a group's link sees the list of names
beside "how do you want to join" once they have picked one.
