# See a group's people as a table on a desktop, with what each paid and where they stand, and a person's page in two columns

Branch: `feat/desktop-people`

Design: https://claude.ai/artifact/Nwc4hfpwGTdcHj4e7suBHQ (board 09 · People, board 10 · A person's page, and the table recipe on 25 · Rules)

From `lg` (1024px) up, People is a table — Person (the name opens their page),
Role, Paid, Share, Position in words, Remind for whoever owes the reader, and a
menu holding the panel the phone opens under a row — with the group link beside
it once the screen has room, and Add someone as the row under it. Paid and Share
come off the balances the screen already loads. A person's page stands in two
columns: the position, its actions and the entries behind it on the left, the
statistics on the right. Below `lg` nothing moves.
