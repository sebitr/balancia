# Lay out a group's statistics, its recurring expenses and its settings for a desktop window

Branch: `feat/desktop-stats-recurring-settings`

Design: https://claude.ai/artifact/Nwc4hfpwGTdcHj4e7suBHQ (board 11 · Statistics, board 12 · Recurring expenses, board 13 · Group settings, and the table recipe on 25 · Rules)

From `lg` (1024px) up, the statistics stand two cards to a row under the four
figures, the recurring expenses are a table — Description, Repeats, Amount,
Next and the row's menu, folding the schedule and the next date under the
name when the table's own box is narrow — with Add at the end of the title's
row, and the group's settings stand in two columns. Below `lg` nothing moves.
Neither statistics screen draws a category in the accent any more.
