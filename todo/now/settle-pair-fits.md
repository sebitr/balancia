# Let a repayment row say both names: stack the figure under the pair instead of cropping the receiver off it

Branch: `fix/settle-pair-fits`

The drawer's Settle tab lists the outstanding debts as "Cyril rembourse
Sébastien" with the figure beside it, on one line that truncated. Two names, a
verb and a five-figure amount do not fit a phone row: the sentence was the only
part that could give ground, so what went was its end — the receiver, which is
the name the reader picks the row by, worst of all in a group where several
rows open with the same payer.

Wrapping the sentence in place fixed the crop and looked terrible: one line
here, a ragged three there, the card's height jumping row to row. So the figure
moved under the sentence instead. The sentence now has the row's whole width
and an ordinary pair never comes near it; every row is two lines of the same
height, and the amounts read down the card as a column of their own.
